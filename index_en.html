Frequently asked questions

Quickly find answers to the most common questions about our services.

How can I consult a doctor via WhatsApp?
Simply click the WhatsApp button on our website, describe your health problem, and a qualified doctor will respond to you as soon as possible to perform your consultation.
Are your doctors really qualified?
Yes, all our doctors are graduates from recognized universities and approved by the Medical Board of DRC. They have also received specialized training in telemedicine.
How does medication delivery work?
After your consultation, if the doctor prescribes medications, you can order them directly via WhatsApp. We deliver them to your home within 24h in Kinshasa and 48h in other cities.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all mobile money payment methods (M-Pesa, Orange Money, Airtel Money) as well as bank transfers and credit cards.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time without fees. The cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
